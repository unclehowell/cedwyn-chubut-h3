# Changelog

## v0.2.2 (7 Nov 2026)
Map usable after zooming in. The tiles were loading (every request HTTP 200, direct from a/b/c.tile.openstreetmap.org, no 403 or policy tile), but this stretch of the Chubut coast is open steppe that OpenStreetMap has not mapped in detail: from zoom 15 every standard tile around the outline is one plain land colour (#F2EFE9, a 103-byte PNG), so the map turned pale and blank after one zoom, with only faint seams between identical tiles. Both maps now carry a Satellite / Street map switch (Leaflet layers control, expanded); the Chubut map opens on Esri World Imagery (`https://server.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer/tile/{z}/{y}/{x}`, already listed in cedwyn `themes.json` `allowed_tile_templates` and used on /maps/; imagery to zoom 17, enlarged beyond, with its attribution), and Llandough keeps the OpenStreetMap layer first. Both layers keep `referrerPolicy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`. Issue #2.

## v0.2.1 (7 Nov 2026)
Map tiles on the live page. The site build keeps a map-tile address only when it starts with a template listed in `allowed_tile_templates` in unclehowell/cedwyn `themes.json` (`https://{s}.tile.openstreetmap.org/`); v0.1.1's bare `https://tile.openstreetmap.org/` form is not listed, so the build rewrote it into a `/wayback/go/?u=` link and the live page loaded no tiles (blocked by the browser). The tile layer is back on the `{s}` form (`subdomains: "abc"`) and keeps `referrerPolicy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, which the tile servers need. Issue #2.

## v0.2.0 (7 Nov 2026)
Own data store (REPO SPLIT, issue #188 on unclehowell/cedwyn; owner rule of 6 Nov: every webapp has its own backend store that pulls only what it needs from the central catalogue). `data/sources.json` lists the two catalogue records the outline rests on (EV-0211, the Stewart survey plan of 1824; EV-0215, the 1841 tithe apportionment). `tools/sync_central.py` (run by `.github/workflows/sync-central.yml` every 6 hours, read-only against `/api/catalogue/<id>`) writes their id, kind, title, date, status and page into `data/central.json`; the page reads only that file. New Records list under “How it was made”, each record linked to its catalogue entry at `/catalogue/?id=`. Footer version and the changelog table brought up to date (the v0.1.1 row was missing).

## v0.1.1 (6 Nov 2026)
Map tiles: the tile images now send the page's origin as the referrer (`referrerPolicy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin` on the tile layer), because the site-wide `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` header stripped it and the OpenStreetMap tile servers refuse requests with no referrer; the deprecated `{s}` subdomains are dropped for the single `tile.openstreetmap.org` host. Issue #2.

## v0.1.0 (3 Nov 2026)
First version. The 43 parcels of the Tithe H3 map (v0.4.0) transposed to the shore of Golfo Nuevo, about 9.7 km south-east of the centre of Puerto Madryn (coast point -42.80441, -64.93000), with the outline's centre 2.65 km inland at right angles to the coast (bearing about 216°), the same distance as the Llandough outline's centre from the sea (nearest OpenStreetMap coastline point: the Cardiff Bay barrage by Penarth Head, 51.45100, -3.16467). Offsets in metres east/north of the centroid (azimuthal equidistant), so size (54.4 ha) and north orientation are kept. Nearest field edge to the sea: 2.00 km at Llandough, 2.05 km on the Chubut coast. H3 resolutions 8 (cell of each parcel centre) and 10 to 13, computed in the browser with h3-js 4.5.0 (centroid-in-polygon); at res 12, 2,302 distinct cells on the Chubut coast against 1,890 at Llandough (cells there are about 234 m² against about 285 m²). A Llandough map shows the same outline and its line to the sea. (i) changelog modal in the footer.
