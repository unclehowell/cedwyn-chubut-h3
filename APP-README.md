# Chubut H3 (section/chubut-h3)
Webapp at /chubut-h3/ and /argentina/chubut-h3/ (Patagonia half, its 2D map). The Cedwyn estate outline from the Tithe H3 map, transposed to the Chubut coast south of Puerto Madryn at the same distance from the sea as at Llandough, true size and north up, in H3 hexagons.
Files: index.html, chubut-h3.css, chubut-h3.js, data/outline.json (both outlines, centres, nearest coast points, distances), vendor/ (Leaflet 1.9.4, h3-js 4.5.0).
Source: tithe-h3 v0.4.0 data/parcels.json; coastline: OpenStreetMap natural=coastline (Overpass, 3 Nov 2026). Argentina scope: no money words.
